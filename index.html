Learn by doing

Build confidence in the basics.

These pages are written for practical learning, not professional certification or safety approval.

CircuitBench focuses on common electronics tasks: checking resistor values, sizing current-limiting resistors, interpreting LED forward voltage, understanding divider loading, and planning safe Arduino and ESP32 connection limits. Each article explains the formula, assumptions, and likely sources of error.

Visitors can use the calculators to estimate values quickly, then use the tutorials to connect those numbers to real wiring and measurement habits. The material works best for low-voltage, beginner-level experiments and design checks that are still verified against component datasheets and the specific hardware in use.
