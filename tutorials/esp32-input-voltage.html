Checking signals before ESP32 inputs

Verify the exact board and module documentation before connecting an external signal to a GPIO.

ESP32-based boards are not interchangeable. GPIO electrical limits, input-only pins, boot-strapping behavior, analog characteristics, and board-level protection vary by chip family and board. Identify the exact module and development board, then read the current technical reference and schematic before wiring.

Measure the signal first

Determine the signal's minimum, maximum, startup, and fault voltage relative to the same ground reference. If the source can be floating or exceed the intended range, consider isolation or a purpose-designed interface instead of directly connecting it. Confirm polarity and transient conditions too.

Divider estimate is not a safety guarantee

A two-resistor divider may reduce a known DC signal. The unloaded equation is Vout = Vin × R₂ ÷ (R₁ + R₂). The input's impedance, resistor tolerance, source impedance, transient behavior, and protection requirements affect the real result. The divider does not clamp overvoltage and cannot handle every signal type.

  1. Verify the GPIO's documented operating and absolute maximum limits for the exact device.
  2. Calculate the worst-case output including supply and resistor tolerances.
  3. Check input current and transient protection, not just nominal voltage.
  4. With power removed, assemble and inspect the interface; use a current-limited test setup when appropriate.
  5. Measure at the input node before connecting the microcontroller if practical.

The voltage divider calculator reports an ideal unloaded value only. Do not interpret it as an ESP32 compatibility checker.

Do not connect unknown or higher-voltage signals directly. GPIO damage can occur even when a board appears to work briefly. Seek qualified review for safety-critical or high-energy interfaces.

Review notes

Specify the tested ESP32 chip and board variant, cite their official datasheets, and validate any example circuit at worst-case values before publication.
