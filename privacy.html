Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Overview

This website is a lightweight static educational resource. It does not currently use a user account system, a server-side database, a contact form, or an advertising SDK. The site is designed to keep calculator inputs local to the browser whenever possible, while still allowing normal web hosting and email communication.

Calculator inputs

Calculator values such as voltage, current, resistance, or LED current are processed by JavaScript in the visitor's browser. These values are not transmitted to a CircuitBench application server and are not stored in a CircuitBench database. This remains true unless the site is changed in a future update to use a backend service or a new third-party script.

Theme preference

If you use the day/night mode control, the selected setting is saved in your browser's local storage under the key circuitbench-theme. This preference is stored on the device, not sent to CircuitBench, and is used only to remember the display setting on future visits. If local storage is unavailable, the page falls back to the device's color-scheme preference for the current visit.

Contact by email

The Contact page contains a mailto link for avinash1919982006@gmail.com. If you send an email, the message is provided to your email client and then transmitted by your chosen email provider. CircuitBench does not operate a contact form or store email messages in a site database. Avoid sending sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable with the handling practices of your email provider and the recipient's mail system.

Hosting-provider and network logs

When a browser requests a page, the hosting provider and network infrastructure may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested URL, timestamps, and similar access logs. This is standard web hosting behavior and is separate from the browser-side calculator processing described above. CircuitBench does not control the retention or use of those logs by the hosting provider, but the site itself does not intentionally collect or store additional personal data through a custom backend.

Advertising and third-party vendors

Current status: CircuitBench does not currently display Google AdSense or other third-party advertising tags. As a result, the site does not currently load advertising vendors or set advertising cookies through those services. No ad personalization or consent-management tools are currently in use.

If advertising is enabled: Google, as well as other advertising vendors used to serve ads, may use cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons or device identifiers) to show and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, detect invalid traffic, and, where enabled, personalize ads based on a user's visits to this site or other sites. These vendors may process information such as IP address, browser or device information, and activity associated with ad requests. The vendors and technologies will depend on the advertising configuration in use. Each vendor handles information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Visitors can review or change Google's ad personalization settings in Google Ads Settings. They can also use the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool and the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out tool to manage interest-based advertising choices for participating companies. These controls do not stop all ads; contextual or non-personalized ads may still appear, and opt-out choices can be browser- or device-specific.

Cookies, identifiers, and related technologies

At present, the site uses only the browser's local storage for the optional dark/light theme preference described above. Because no advertising tags are active, CircuitBench does not currently use ad cookies or similar technologies through advertising vendors. If advertising is enabled, those vendors may store or access cookies or similar technologies in a visitor's browser; visitors can manage or remove stored cookies through browser settings, though blocking them may affect advertising functionality.

For details about Google's advertising technologies and privacy practices, see Google's Advertising Technologies and the privacy policy of any other advertising vendor used.

Your choices and questions

You can clear local storage data for this site in your browser settings to remove the saved theme preference. You may also disable or restrict cookies, but that will not affect the browser-side calculator behavior itself. If you have questions about the site or want to report a problem, please use the email contact on the Contact page.

Policy updates

This privacy policy is intended to match the current site configuration. If the website adds advertising, analytics, forms, or other data-collection features, the policy will be revised to describe the actual behavior and the implementation date. This page is informational and should be reviewed alongside applicable local privacy laws and hosting terms.
