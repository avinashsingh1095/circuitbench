Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Information entered in calculators

Calculator inputs are processed by JavaScript in your browser. This site does not send those values to a CircuitBench application server or store them in a CircuitBench database. The calculations run locally on the page.

Theme preference

If you use the day/night mode control, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key circuitbench-theme so the preference can be remembered on later visits. This preference stays on your device and is not sent to CircuitBench. If local storage is unavailable, the page uses your device's color-scheme preference for the current visit.

Contact by email

The Contact page links to avinash1919982006@gmail.com using a mailto link. Selecting it opens your email application; the site itself does not provide a message form or receive the message. If you choose to email, your message and email address are handled by your email provider and the recipient's email service. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Advertising and third-party vendors

Current status: CircuitBench does not currently display advertisements or use Google AdSense. The conditional information below describes what may apply if advertising is enabled on this site; this policy must be revised to match the actual ad products, vendors, consent controls, and settings before ads are launched.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is added, Google and its advertising partners/vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, browser/device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, and, depending on your settings and location, personalize ads. Third-party vendors may collect or receive information about your visits to this and other websites to provide those services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms; CircuitBench does not control their technologies or retention practices. The specific vendors and data uses will depend on the advertising configuration in use.

If personalized Google advertising is available, you can review or change it in Google My Ad Center. You can also manage or clear cookies in your browser and use advertising choice tools such as aboutads.info choices or Your Online Choices where available. Blocking or deleting cookies may limit some advertising technologies, but may not prevent all ad selection or measurement; you may still see contextual or non-personalized ads. Where required, the site will provide an appropriate consent mechanism before using advertising technologies that require consent.

Forms, accounts, and other tracking

This package has no account system, contact submission form, or analytics script. It currently contains no advertising code, intentionally set cookies, or third-party runtime library. Apart from the optional theme preference described above, the site does not use local storage. If advertising or other data collection is enabled later, this policy and the live consent/configuration controls must be updated before that change is published.

Hosting and network data

When a browser requests a static page, the hosting provider and network intermediaries may process technical information such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, browser headers, and security logs. Their collection, retention, and use are governed by their own terms and privacy policies; CircuitBench does not control those services.

Your choices and questions

You can clear the saved theme preference using your browser's site data controls or leave it unchanged using the device preference. Browser controls may also limit local storage or network access. For questions or to report an issue, use the email on the Contact page.

Policy changes

If site features or hosting practices change, update this page to describe the actual behavior and the effective date. This informational text is not legal advice and may need review for the operator's jurisdiction.
