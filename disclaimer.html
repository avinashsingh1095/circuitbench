Engineering and educational disclaimer

Use this site as a learning aid, not as a substitute for authoritative design review.

Verify every result

Calculators implement simplified equations under the assumptions stated on their pages. Inputs, units, rounding, component behavior, load conditions, tolerance, temperature, and numeric limits can affect a real design. Check calculations independently and compare them with current manufacturer datasheets and board documentation.

Draft tutorial content

All ten tutorial articles are original drafts that require factual review and practical testing before publication or use. They do not claim to cover every board, component, software version, jurisdiction, or operating condition. Examples are educational illustrations, not verified instructions for a particular build.
