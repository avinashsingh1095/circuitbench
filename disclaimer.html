Engineering and educational disclaimer

Use this site as a learning aid and design sanity check, not as a substitute for proper engineering review, manufacturer data, or safety testing.

Calculator assumptions

The calculators are based on simplified equations and common assumptions: steady DC conditions, idealized resistor behavior, a single LED with a fixed forward voltage at the target current, unloaded divider calculations, and basic power relationships. Real components can vary with temperature, tolerance, supply variation, soldering quality, wiring resistance, battery condition, and load changes.

For example, a resistor's actual value may differ from its nominal tolerance, an LED may not match the assumed forward drop at the intended current, and a divider may produce a different output once connected to a circuit that draws current. The calculator outputs should therefore be treated as estimates that need checking against the specific component and board design.

Educational scope

The tutorial articles are intended to teach foundational concepts in accessible language. They are not a complete safety review, product certification, or manufacturer-approved design package. Some examples use simplified circuits and general formulas that are helpful for learning but must be verified against the exact board, microcontroller, sensor, actuator, or power source used in a real project.

Electrical safety

Electronics can pose risks including electric shock, burns, fire, and equipment damage. CircuitBench does not provide instructions for mains wiring, high-voltage work, safety-critical systems, or installations that require licensed professional design. Always follow local electrical codes and use appropriately rated tools, connectors, wire gauges, enclosures, and protection measures. When working on real hardware, disconnect power before changing wiring, confirm current limits, and stop if a part or circuit is not clearly understood.

No warranty

CircuitBench is provided for general educational and reference purposes. There is no guarantee of perfection, completeness, fitness for a particular purpose, or suitability for a specific project. Nothing on this site creates an engineering consulting relationship or professional design approval. Use the site responsibly, check your assumptions, and consult the relevant datasheets, device documentation, and qualified professionals where necessary.
