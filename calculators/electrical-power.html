Electrical power calculator

Enter exactly two known positive DC values, leave the other two blank, and select which result to solve for.

Result

Enter two known values and select Calculate to see a result.

Formula and assumptions

For a resistive DC load, use P = V × I, P = I²R, or P = V² ÷ R, with Ohm's law connecting the quantities. Inputs must be exactly two known values; the chosen output must be left blank. These equations do not account for AC power factor, changing load behavior, or efficiency.

Power calculated for a component is not the same as a safe rating. Check the datasheet, temperature derating, airflow, and design margin.

Worked example

A resistive load with 12 V across it and 2 A through it dissipates P = 12 × 2 = 24 W. Confirm the load and source can operate at those values and that all components are rated for the resulting heat.
