Calculator 04

Four-band resistor color code

Select the bands in order: first significant digit, second significant digit, multiplier, then tolerance. This tool decodes four-band resistors only.

Result

Select four bands and choose Decode bands.

How the bands work

The first two bands form a two-digit number. Multiply by the third band's factor to get nominal resistance; the fourth band states the tolerance. The calculator shows the resulting minimum-to-maximum nominal range. Band conventions can vary for components outside the four-band scheme.

Worked example

Brown, black, red, gold means 10 × 100 = 1,000 Ω (1 kΩ), with ±5% tolerance. Its nominal range is 950 Ω to 1,050 Ω. Inspect the actual component and its datasheet when exact identification matters.

Color digit reference

Black 0, brown 1, red 2, orange 3, yellow 4, green 5, blue 6, violet 7, gray 8, white 9. Gold and silver are used here for multiplier or tolerance bands, not significant digits.
