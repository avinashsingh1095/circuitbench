Calculator 02

LED series resistor calculator

Estimate a resistor for one LED on a DC supply. The estimate assumes a fixed LED forward voltage and ignores other series drops and changing operating conditions.

Use the LED datasheet's forward voltage at the intended current. Do not assume every LED has the same rating.

Result

Enter values and select Calculate to see a result.

Formula and assumptions

For a single series LED, estimate R = (Vsupply − Vf) ÷ I. Convert current from milliamperes to amperes before using the formula. Resistor dissipation at the ideal resistance is P = I²R. The suggested standard value is rounded upward from an E12 series set so the estimated current does not exceed the target in this ideal model.

This does not establish that the current is safe for a particular LED or GPIO. Check the LED and driver datasheets, supply tolerance, resistor power rating, and any microcontroller pin-current limits. For multiple LEDs, use a circuit-specific analysis.

Worked example

With a 5 V supply, a 2.0 V LED forward drop, and a 10 mA target, R = (5 − 2) ÷ 0.010 = 300 Ω. The next selected E12 value is 330 Ω. The ideal estimate at 330 Ω is about 9.09 mA; actual current varies with the LED and supply.
