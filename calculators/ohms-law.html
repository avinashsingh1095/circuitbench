Formula and assumptions

Ohm's law is V = I × R, where voltage is in volts (V), current is in amperes (A), and resistance is in ohms (Ω). Rearrangements are I = V ÷ R and R = V ÷ I. The displayed power is calculated as P = V × I.

Use this basic form for an approximately ohmic load under a steady DC operating condition. It does not model changing resistance, AC phase, or nonlinear devices such as LEDs.

Worked example

A 9 V source supplies 0.020 A through a resistor. R = 9 ÷ 0.020 = 450 Ω; power is 9 × 0.020 = 0.18 W. A real design should account for resistor tolerance and choose a suitable power rating with margin.
